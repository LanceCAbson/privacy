Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Surname Story ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application on iOS and Android.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal detail, and the App has no account to sign up for. Most of what the App remembers is kept on your own device. The exceptions are the surname you search for, a random install identifier, and a record of which surnames a purchase has unlocked, all described below. The App has no advertising and does not track you.

The surname you search for

When you search, the surname you type is sent to our service, which is hosted on Google Firebase (Google Cloud) in London. Our service asks OpenAI to write the report for that surname, and sends OpenAI only the surname itself, not your device identifiers or anything else about you. The finished report is stored on our service under the surname, so that the next person who searches for the same surname is served the same report. A stored report is not linked to you or to your device. The surname you type is never used for advertising.

A random install identifier

The first time you search, the App makes a random identifier on your device and sends it with each search. It is not an advertising identifier and is not linked to your name, email address or any account. We use it only to count how many new reports one installation asks for, so that no single device can run up our costs. These counters expire on their own. If an installation sends no identifier, we count by the IP address of the request instead, in the same way. A fresh identifier is made if you reinstall the App.

Stored on your device only

The App keeps a copy of reports you have opened in the storage of the app on your device, so reopening one is instant and a report you have unlocked can be read without a connection. The PDF report is made on your device; it goes nowhere unless you choose to save or share it with your phone's share sheet. All of this is removed when you uninstall the App.

What we do not collect

We do not track your location, read your contacts, photos or files, or ask for anything that identifies you as a person. The App does not currently include analytics or crash-reporting tools.

2. Third-Party Services

Report writing – OpenAI

Reports are written by an AI model run by OpenAI. It receives the surname you searched for and nothing else. Reports are written from general historical knowledge and may contain mistakes, especially for rare surnames: they are a starting point for your own research, not a genealogical record.

OpenAI Privacy Policy

Hosting – Google Firebase

Our service and the stored reports are hosted on Google Firebase (Cloud Functions and Cloud Firestore). Google processes this data on our behalf as our hosting provider, and may log technical information such as your IP address as part of serving requests.

Firebase Privacy Policy

Purchases (optional)

The App offers one optional purchase, which unlocks the full report for two surnames of your choice, and can be bought again for more. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we receive no payment details and no information about who bought it. To unlock a report, the App sends the store's proof of purchase (an Apple signed transaction or a Google Play purchase token) to our service, which checks it with Apple's signature or with Google Play. So that one purchase unlocks no more than two surnames, our service keeps the store's transaction number (for Google Play, a one-way scrambled form of the purchase token) and the surnames it has unlocked. This record does not contain your name, email address, store account or device identifiers. The App also keeps your proofs of purchase on your device so it can show your unlocked reports again, and you can restore them on a new device with the same store account.

Fonts and maps

The App's typefaces and the world map are built into the App, so nothing is requested from a font or map service when you use it.

App Store Platforms

The App is distributed through Apple (App Store) and Google (Play Store). These platforms may collect usage and device information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store payment information.

3. No Advertising or Tracking

The App shows no advertisements, contains no advertising or analytics software, and does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android). It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your data.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data held on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App. A stored report is kept on our service without any link to you; because we cannot tell which reports were asked for by whom, there is nothing to delete about you there. Usage counters described above expire on their own within a day. The purchase record described above is kept for as long as the App is offered, so that your unlocked surnames can be restored. Data collected by the third-party services above is retained according to their own policies, linked above.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights over your personal data, including access, correction, and deletion. We hold very little that can be tied to you; contact us using the details below and we will act on anything we can find. For data held by Apple, Google or OpenAI, please use their privacy links above.
