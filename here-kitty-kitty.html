Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Here Kitty Kitty ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application on iOS and Android.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you for your name, email address, or any other personal detail, and the App has no account to sign up for. Most of what the App remembers is kept on your own device. The exceptions are the optional daily leaderboard, the anonymous usage measurement, and the advertising described below.

Stored on your device only

The App saves your play record in the browser storage of the app on your device: which cats you have found and how often, your times for each night's house, hints used, your chosen colour palette, and the display name you picked for the leaderboard. This data never leaves your device unless you submit a score, and it is removed when you uninstall the App.

The daily leaderboard (optional)

Everybody plays the same generated house each day, and the App can rank your time against other players. Nothing is submitted unless you choose a display name. If you do, the following is sent to our Google Firebase project and is visible to other players in the in-game Ranks list for that day's house:

  • the display name you typed (up to 16 characters — choose a nickname, not your real name, if you would rather not be identified)
  • your completion time in seconds and the number of hints you used
  • the time you finished
  • an anonymous account identifier (see below)

Leaving the name blank means you never appear on the board and nothing is sent.

Anonymous sign-in

To make sure one player cannot overwrite another player's row, the App creates an anonymous Firebase account the first time it contacts the leaderboard. It is not a login: there is no email address, no password, and nothing that identifies you. It is a random identifier stored on your device, and a fresh one is created if you reinstall the App.

What we do not collect

We do not track your location, read your contacts, photos, or files, or ask for anything that identifies you as a person. The usage measurement described below counts what happens in the game; it is not tied to your name and is not used to work out who you are.

2. Third-Party Services

Analytics – Google Firebase

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is actually played — sessions started, houses finished, how long a night takes, how often a hint is asked for, which screens are opened. Firebase may collect device identifiers, usage data, and crash reports. No personally identifiable information is collected, and nothing you type is sent as part of it.

  • Firebase Privacy Policy

Leaderboard hosting – Google Firebase

The leaderboard uses Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in) and Cloud Firestore to store and serve the scores described above. Google processes this data on our behalf as our hosting provider, and may log technical information such as your IP address as part of serving those requests.

  • Firebase Privacy Policy

Advertising – Google AdMob

The App displays banner and interstitial advertisements, and offers optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob. We use no other ad network and no mediation platform. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android), IP address, and other non-personal technical information in order to serve and measure advertisements.

On iOS we request your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking. In the UK, the EEA, and other regions where it is required, we show Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before any ad is requested, and you can reopen that form at any time from the Credits screen inside the App to change your choice.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from AdMob

App Store Platforms

The App is distributed through Apple (App Store) and Google (Play Store). These platforms may collect usage and device information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store payment information.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Privacy Policy

3. iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14.5 and later, we ask for your permission before your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for personalised advertising. You may deny this request and the App will continue to function normally; ads will simply be less personalised. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information — including a display name on the leaderboard — please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data held on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App. Leaderboard entries stay on that day's board so the ranking remains accurate, and cannot be deleted from inside the App. If you want an entry removed, email us with the display name and the date you played and we will delete it. Data collected by the third-party services above is retained according to their own policies, linked above.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights over your personal data, including access, correction, and deletion. For anything we hold — in practice, a leaderboard entry — contact us using the details below and we will act on it. For data held by Google as an advertising business, please use the Google links above.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
