Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sleep like a Baby: White Noise ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application on iOS and Android.

In short. There is no account and no sign-up. Sleep and health data that the App reads on your phone (from Apple Health, Health Connect and your phone's motion sensors) stays on your phone: we do not send it to our servers, to analytics, to advertisers or to anyone else, and we never sell it. A few simple, anonymous usage details go to Google Firebase, and the free version shows ads. Details are below.

1. Information the App uses

Health and sleep data (read on your device only)

If you choose to turn on sleep tracking, the App can read the following, always with your permission, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings:

  • Apple Health (iOS): your sleep analysis from a wearable, read-only. The App does not write to Apple Health.
  • Health Connect (Android): your sleep sessions (the "read sleep" permission), read-only. The App does not write to Health Connect.
  • Motion and activity (iOS Motion & Fitness; Android activity recognition and Google's Sleep API): your phone's motion and step history, to estimate when you fell asleep and woke, so you can track your sleep without a wearable.
  • Screen on/off history (Android, usage access): used the same way, to estimate when you went to bed and woke up.

We use this data only to show your weekly sleep pattern and your last night inside the App. It is stored on your device. It is not sent to us, to Google Firebase or any other analytics service, to ad networks, or to any other third party; it is not used for advertising; and it is not sold. Removing the App removes the App's copy. Your data in Apple Health and Health Connect is not changed.

Your answers to the setup questions

When you first open the App it asks a few optional questions (for example what makes sleep hard, which noise keeps you up, when bedtime usually starts, and which sounds you use now). You can skip them. Your answers are kept on your device and are used to choose sounds and your sleep coach's nightly steps. Because the answers describe sleep difficulties, we treat them as health information. A short coded version of your answers (for example "noise" and "snoring") is sent to Google Firebase Analytics as anonymous user properties, tied to a random app-install identifier and not to your name, email or any account, so we can see which choices are common and whether the App helps. We do not use these answers to target advertising and we do not give them to ad networks.

Your sleep coach

The coach is a set of fixed, pre-written nightly steps that live inside the App. Choosing the steps and writing your reminder happen on your device. We log simple events (for example that you opened the coach, which night of the plan it was, and whether you answered "helped" or "not really") to Google Firebase Analytics, with no sleep times or measurements. You can turn the coach off in Settings.

Bedtime stories

If you ask for a bedtime story, the topic and the optional name you type are sent to our story service (running on Google Cloud), which passes them to OpenAI to write the story. A random per-install code, not your device identifier, is sent with each request only so we can limit how often one install can ask. Please do not type personal details. Requests can remain in our server logs and with OpenAI for a limited period for debugging, abuse prevention and as OpenAI's terms allow. We do not link them to your identity. The story is read aloud with your phone's built-in text-to-speech voice or an on-device voice; the App does not record audio.

Suggestions (optional)

If you choose to send a suggestion from the Settings screen, the text you write is sent to our server, which is hosted by Cloudflare, and stored there together with the name of the game, your platform, the app version, the language the App is using, the country your connection came from, and a random identifier the App made up for this install. That identifier is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your name, email address or any account. We read the suggestions people send, and may use a translation tool to read ones written in a language we do not speak. To limit how many messages one device can send, a scrambled (hashed) copy of your IP address is kept for about two days; we do not store the IP address itself. We do not ask for your name or email address and cannot reply, so please do not put personal details in a suggestion. Suggestions are not used for advertising and are not shared with ad networks. We keep them until we delete them; to have one removed, contact us using the details at the end of this policy, quoting its text, and we will delete it.

2. Third-Party Services

Analytics and crash reports – Google Firebase

We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to fix crashes. They may collect device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, crash logs), a random app-instance identifier, advertising identifier where you allow it, and usage events (for example which sounds are played and how long a session lasts). No name, email or sleep measurement is collected.

  • Firebase Privacy Policy

Advertising – LevelPlay Mediation (IronSource)

The free version of the App displays ads served through the LevelPlay mediation platform, which includes the following ad networks:

  • IronSource / Unity Ads
  • Google AdMob

These networks may collect device advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android), IP address, and other technical information to serve and measure advertisements. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions we ask for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads. On iOS, we request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing your IDFA. We do not give ad networks your health data, sleep data or your answers to the setup questions.

  • IronSource / LevelPlay Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (for example a Premium subscription) processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not receive or store your payment information; we only learn whether Premium is active. Purchases are governed by the respective platform's terms and privacy policy.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Privacy Policy

Notifications

The App may ask permission to send local notifications (for example a bedtime reminder, which can include your coach's step for the night). They are created and delivered on your device and do not transmit personal data to us.

Settings and catalogue files

The App downloads small public files (feature settings and a list of our other apps) from GitHub Pages. Like any web request this reveals your IP address to the host; no personal data is sent in it.

3. iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14.5 and later, we ask for your permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalised advertising. You may deny this request and the App will continue to function normally; ads will simply be less personalised.

4. Health information and medical advice

The App is for relaxation and general wellbeing. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you have ongoing trouble sleeping, new or sudden ringing in your ears, snoring with pauses in breathing, or worries about your baby's sleep, please talk to a doctor.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences, including parents looking for sounds for a baby's sleep. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data on your device (sleep history, setup answers, coach progress, saved mixes) stays there until you remove the App or clear its data. We do not run user accounts. Data held by third-party services (Firebase, ad networks, OpenAI) is retained according to their own policies, linked above. Because we do not know who you are, we cannot find your data by name; if you would like anything removed, contact us and we will help where we can.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location you may have rights regarding your personal data, including access, correction, and deletion. You can also: withdraw Health, Motion or notification permissions in your phone's settings; skip or retake the setup questions; turn the coach off in Settings; reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's privacy settings; and change your ad consent choice in the App. Please contact the relevant third-party service directly, or contact us using the details below and we will assist where possible.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
